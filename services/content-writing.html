GOT QUESTIONS?

A few things
to know.

Here are some common questions about our content writing services. Have something else in mind? Get in touch with us.

What types of content do you write?

We offer social media captions, blog articles, website copy, video scripts, LinkedIn content, email copywriting, product descriptions and proofreading.

Can you follow my brand voice?

Yes. Share your brand guidelines, preferred tone or existing content so we can understand the style you're aiming for.

Do you write SEO-friendly articles?

We can structure articles around relevant topics, search intent, headings and readability. Search rankings cannot be guaranteed.

How do I start a project?

Contact us with your content requirements, intended audience and project goals. We'll discuss the scope and next steps.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the content type, scope and project requirements. Contact us to discuss your project and request a quote.

Can I request changes?

Revision requirements can be discussed and agreed upon before the project begins. This helps set clear expectations for both sides.
